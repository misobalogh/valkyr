# Ideas

Raw notes, not decisions. Anything that becomes a hard decision moves into an
ADR in `docs/adr/`.

## Theme

- Vikings and Norse mythology. Legendary gear named after myth: Mjolnir,
  Gungnir and similar.
- Roughly ARK meets Drakensang, with a Norse setting.
- Survival, monster fighting, base building and defending against ice and dark
  monsters, combined with Diablo/Drakensang style looting, gearing and crafting.
- Dungeons and caves for loot runs.

## Movement and armor

- Warframe style, extremely fast and fluid. Reference point: Gauss, the
  kinetic speed frame.
- Armor is part of the movement kit, not just a stat stick. Different sets
  should change how a character moves (sprint, slide, wall-run, glide).

## Combat

- Assassin's Creed Valhalla feel: weighty melee, stagger, stealth kills,
  ranged options, shield and weapon combos.

## Builds

- Question: classes? Leaning towards no fixed classes.
- Identity comes from the armor being worn and what the player has upgraded at
  the moment, plus race. Respeccing is just swapping gear and investing upgrades.

## Races

- Dwarf, elf, human.
- More are possible, undecided.
- Races feed the same modifier pipeline as gear (see M2).

## Crafting

- Definitely in. Recipe graph with stations, raw-material cost roll-up and
  loot that feeds crafting.

## Mounts and creatures

- Combat owls that can be flown, with armor in the style of the owl warriors
  from Legend of the Guardians. Several variants:
  - scout, similar to the ARK light saddle setup
  - fighter, armored for combat
  - more variants later
- Wolves, bears, rock drakes and Managarmr (the moon-hound of Norse myth).
- Mounts use the same loadout pipeline as characters.

## Open questions

- How many races at launch and what does each one change mechanically?
- How much of the speed kit is gated behind armor sets versus available to all?
- Does flying on an owl replace the glide, or sit on top of it?
- How is taming paced, and do mounts have their own gear chase?
- Base defense: how often do waves hit, and are they scheduled or reactive?
